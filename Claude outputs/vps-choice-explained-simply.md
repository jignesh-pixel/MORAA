# Which Computer Should Run MORAA GemVision?

### 1. In One Line (The "Big Picture")
We're renting a little computer to run our shop, and the best one is the one that lives right next door to our notebook.

### 2. The Toybox Story (How It Works)
Our shop keeps a big notebook of every customer and every rupee. That notebook lives in a house in Mumbai. Every time a customer sends a message, our helper runs to the notebook, many times, one after another.

If the helper lives in the same Mumbai house (AWS Lightsail Mumbai), each trip takes a blink. If he lives in Bangalore (DigitalOcean), each trip is a short bus ride. If he lives in Singapore (Hetzner), each trip is a long flight. Hostinger is a cheap helper who might live nearby, but he wants you to pay for years up front, and he's not as dependable. All of them cost about the same, so pick the neighbour.

### 3. Why It Matters to You
- **Pick AWS Lightsail Mumbai:** customers get faster replies, for about $12 a month.
- **Size:** start with 2 GB of memory, which is the minimum. Move up to 4 GB ($24 a month) so the shop can serve more customers at once.
